🔗 Group Compositions

Groups can be combined through various operations:

Direct Product (G × H)

Creates a new group whose elements are ordered pairs (g,h). The order is |G| × |H|.

Example: C₂ × C₃ ≅ C₆ (cyclic group of order 6)

Semidirect Product (G ⋊ H)

A more general construction that incorporates group actions.

Example: S₃ ≅ C₃ ⋊ C₂ (symmetric group on 3 elements)

📚 Mathematical Background

The Classification Theorem

Every finite simple group belongs to one of these categories:

  1. Cyclic groups of prime order
  2. Alternating groups Aₙ for n ≥ 5
  3. Groups of Lie type (classical and exceptional)
  4. Sporadic groups (26 exceptional cases)
  5. Twisted Lie type groups

🎯 Interactive Features

This atlas provides a visual representation of finite simple groups as tiles that can be composed into larger structures. Each tile represents a unique group with properties encoded in its size, color, and position.

  • Tile size: Represents the logarithm of group order
  • Color: Indicates the group family
  • Position: Shows relationships and complexity
  • Connections: Show group compositions
